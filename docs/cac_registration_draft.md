## 2. Objects of the Company (Memorandum of Association)

The objects for which the Company is established are:

1.  **To carry on the business of research, development, and deployment of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Natural Language Processing (NLP) systems**, with a specific focus on low-resource language technologies, localized software solutions, and speech synthesis architectures.
2.  **To engage in applied computational linguistics research, speech engineering, and digital corpus curation**, including the development of advanced Grapheme-to-Phoneme pipelines, phonetic normalizations, and tonal mapping systems for African languages.
3.  **To design, build, test, license, sell, and deploy software applications, developer toolkits, Software Development Kits (SDKs), Application Programming Interfaces (APIs)**, and database architectures for cloud-based, local-first, and edge-native environments.
4.  **To provide consulting, advisory, auditing, and training services** to private corporations, corporate entities, non-governmental organizations (NGOs), and government departments on software engineering, technology integration, artificial intelligence alignment, and linguistic localization.
5.  **To manufacture, assemble, import, export, distribute, and sell computer hardware, internet-of-things (IoT) devices, and offline-compatible electronic systems** pre-configured with speech synthesis and local language translation software.
6.  **To enter into partnerships, joint ventures, and research collaborations** with academic institutions, research councils, technology firms, and venture capitals globally to advance scientific research in African language computing.
7.  **To do all such other things as may be considered incidental or conducive** to the attainment of the above objects or any of them under the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) of Nigeria.
